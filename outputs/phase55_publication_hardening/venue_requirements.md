# Phase 5.5 — Target Venue Technical & Formatting Requirements

**Document Date:** 2026-10-14 11:01:29  

---

## Primary Target: Computers and Electronics in Agriculture (Elsevier)

- **Article Type:** Full Length Research Paper

- **Word Count Guidance:** Typically 6,000 to 9,000 words excluding references and tables.

- **Structure:** Title, Abstract (max 250 words), Highlights (3-5 bullet points, max 85 characters each), Keywords (4-6), Introduction, Materials and Methods, Results, Discussion, Conclusion, References.

- **Artwork Guidelines:** High resolution (min 300 DPI for photographs, 600 DPI for line art/plots), EPS or TIFF/PNG format.

- **Reproducibility Mandate:** Data availability statement and code link required.


## Secondary Target: IEEE Transactions on AgriFood Electronics (T-AFE)

- **Format:** Two-column IEEE Transactions LaTeX template (`IEEEtran.cls`).

- **Page Limit:** Regular papers are typically 8 to 10 pages; excess page charges apply beyond 10 pages.

- **Hardware Focus:** Must emphasize computational efficiency, edge deployment, and inference latency on commodity hardware.

- **Open Science:** IEEE Xplore code/dataset hosting via IEEE DataPort or public GitHub repository.


## Pre-Submission Formatting Checklist

- [x] Abstract bounded to 250 words without citations.

- [x] All 6 high-resolution figures formatted with professional serif typography at 300 DPI.

- [x] Complete BibTeX bibliography with valid DOIs.

- [x] Disclaimers for unvalidated robotic hardware and background causal attribution explicitly included in Limitations.
